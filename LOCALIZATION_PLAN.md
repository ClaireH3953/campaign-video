# 鹽埕區慈愛里 Campaign Video Localization Plan

## Target Location: 鹽埕區慈愛里
- **Address**: 高雄市鹽埕區北端街30號
- **Population**: 361 people, 162 households
- **Demographics**: 27.4% elderly (65+), severe aging problem
- **Village Chief**: 呂佳穎 (Lü Jia-ying)
- **Critical Issue**: Will merge with 博愛里 to become "北端里" on July 1, 2026

## Key Community Issues to Address in Video

### 1. Aging Population Crisis
- 27.4% of residents are 65+
- Need for elder care services
- Accessibility improvements needed

### 2. Population Decline
- -12.38% population loss over 10 years
- Young families leaving the area
- Need revitalization to attract residents back

### 3. Village Merger
- Historic "慈愛里" name will disappear on July 1, 2026
- Residents facing identity crisis
- Need strong advocacy during transition

### 4. Declining Commercial District
- Old commercial area losing vitality
- Need economic revitalization
- Support for local businesses

## Local Landmarks for Photos

1. **愛河 (Love River)** - The iconic landmark, flows through 鹽埕區
   - Location: Along the western edge of 鹽埕區慈愛里
   - Keywords: 愛河 鹽埕 河岸 親水公園
   - Photo opportunities: River views, bridges, riverside parks

2. **仁德公園 (Rende Park)** / **府北公園**
   - Location: Near 大有街
   - Keywords: 鹽埕 仁德公園 府北公園 綠地
   - Photo opportunities: Park spaces, elderly activities, community gatherings

3. **北端街 Local Streets**
   - Location: Main street in 慈愛里
   - Keywords: 鹽埕區 北端街 街景 社區
